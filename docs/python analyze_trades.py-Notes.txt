Then paste report.txt into Claude along with the raw CSV and ask 
something like "Look at the report summary and the underlying 
trades — what patterns does the report miss?" 

Giving both the aggregated report AND the raw data matters: 
the report gives Claude the framing, the raw CSV lets it actually 
verify or dig deeper.
One caveat worth naming: Claude will find patterns whether they're 
real or not. With 20 trades, any "pattern" Claude spots is a 
hypothesis to test, not a conclusion. The script's 3-trade and 
30-trade warnings apply even harder to narrative claims, because 
stories are more convincing than numbers even when they 
shouldn't be.
