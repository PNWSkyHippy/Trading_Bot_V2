Main options
Order quantity
Number of contracts entered. Use 1 for your current test.

TIF (Time in Force)
DAY cancels unfilled working orders at the session/broker cutoff. GTC keeps them working until filled or manually cancelled. For learning in Sim101, DAY is simpler.

Parameter type
Determines what the stop and target numbers mean:
Ticks: distance in minimum price increments.
Price: exact target/stop price.
Currency: dollar distance from entry.
Percent: percentage from entry.
Pips: mainly forex.

Target 1 – Quantity
Contracts managed by this stop/target pair. With one entry contract, use 1.

Stop loss
Distance from your filled entry. For EMT, enter 8 ticks.

Profit
Profit-target distance. For EMT, enter 8 ticks. A value of 0 means you do not have the desired two-point target.

Stop strategy
Optional automatic stop management:
None: fixed stop.
Custom/template: can move to breakeven or trail automatically.

add / remove
Adds or removes targets. For example, with two contracts you could exit one at eight ticks and another at sixteen. You only need one target currently.

“More” options
Reverse at stop
When stopped out, NinjaTrader immediately opens a position in the opposite direction. Leave this off.

Reverse at target
After reaching the target, it immediately enters in the opposite direction. Leave this off.

Target chase
If the target is touched but not filled, NinjaTrader moves the target toward the market. This can get you filled at a worse price. Leave off initially.

Chase limit (t)
Maximum number of ticks an order is allowed to chase. It matters only when a chase option is enabled.

Chase
Moves a limit entry toward the market when price moves away, up to the chase limit. This can turn a patient limit order into a less favorable entry.

Chase if touch
Chase begins only after the market first touches your limit price.

Stop limit for stop loss
Uses a stop-limit instead of a stop-market order. A stop-limit can trigger but remain unfilled during a fast move, leaving the position exposed. Leave unchecked while learning.

MIT for profit
Uses a Market-if-Touched order for the target instead of a limit order. It improves the chance of exiting after the target is touched, but the fill can slip beyond the intended price.

Shadow strategy
Runs an alternate simulated trade-management plan alongside another strategy for comparison. You don’t need it for the current Sim101 EMT test.

Save as Template
Saves these settings so you can select them again. A useful name would be EMT-1-Contract-2Point.

OK / Cancel
OK accepts the ATM settings; Cancel discards the changes.

NinjaTrader’s official references cover the ATM parameters, advanced options, and automatic stop strategies.
