Exactly. The math gets brutal fast:

2 params × 75 values each  =     5,625 combos
3 params × 75 values each  =   421,875 combos
4 params × 75 values each  = 31,640,625 combos
5 params × 75 values each  = 2.37 BILLION combos
At 2 seconds per backtest, 5 params step=1 is 150 years of compute.

Zoom collapses it:

Stage 1 coarse (6 buckets):  7^5  =     16,807  → ~9 hours
Stage 2 fine   (±3 window):  7^5  =     16,807  → ~9 hours
Total:  ~18 hours vs 150 years
And you still get the same answer because the coarse pass eliminates the 99.9% of the space that's obviously bad before you ever waste fine-step time on it. The only risk is if two params have a weird interaction where the global optimum is hidden in a valley between coarse sample points — that's when you'd crank --zoom-coarse-buckets up to 10 or 12 before drilling in. But for most strategy params that behave monotonically in some direction, 6 buckets finds the right neighborhood every time.
