In breakout logic, do not assume an upward move means long continuation.
A parabolic rise often transitions into a parabolic drop if the signal is late.
Direction must be based on explicit signal.direction first, not inferred from positive price movement alone.
If direction is unavailable, only fall back to momentum_score as a backup.
Late/exhausted breakout states should be rejected rather than auto-longed.
Only consider shorting a failed upside breakout when reversal is confirmed, not merely because price paused after rising.
Audit breakoutscanner.py for any remaining places that still infer LONG/SHORT from momentum_score instead of signal.direction, especially:

WatchlistManager.check()
classify_signal()
ScannerGUI.add_alert()
any CSV tracking / escalation logic
