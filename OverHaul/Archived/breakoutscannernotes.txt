divergence_pct = abs(exec_price - scanner_price) / scanner_price * 100

Because:

BTC/USD
BTC/USDT

D. Preferred execution venue per asset
For example:

BTC, ETH, SOL -> Coinbase
certain alts -> Kraken
stocks -> IBKR/Alpaca
scanner may still observe others, but final decision uses preferred venue
Why this matters to your current breakout pipeline
Your breakout scanner is exactly where this can go wrong:

mover detected on one venue
history from another
receiver re-verifies from another
execution maybe from another
That can create:

false breakouts
bad wick checks
invalid structural stops
phantom momentum
weird “it backtested but live failed” behavior
So your instinct here is dead right.

My recommendation
For crypto, I’d move toward:

Conservative model
Use aggregate sources only to find candidates
Use one chosen venue for actual trade decisions
For example:

CoinGecko / multi-exchange scan flags ARB/USD
Bot says: okay, if we trade ARB, our venue is Coinbase
Pull Coinbase-only price/history
Re-score there
Only then allow trade
That’s much safer than mixing venues.
