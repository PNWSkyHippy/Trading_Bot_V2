## Highest-priority new diagnostic: exit counterfactuals

Do NOT assume `early_loss` is a leak just because it is common.

Add a diagnostic analyzer for all trades closed by:
- `early_loss`
- `early_loss_no_data`
- `perf_time_stop_1hr`
- `perf_time_stop_3hr`
- `hard_time_stop`

For each such trade, compute and export:
- actual exit pnl
- pnl 1 bar later
- pnl 3 bars later
- pnl 6 bars later
- whether structural stop would have been hit later
- whether take profit would have been hit later

Goal:
Determine whether these exits are:
- protecting capital correctly
- or cutting trades that would have recovered

This should be exportable to CSV.
