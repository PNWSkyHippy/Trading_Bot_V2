Yeah exactly — for enterprise/business use that pipeline is almost perfect:

PI produces a documented design spec with exact layout, components, behavior — no ambiguity
Claude implements against that spec — less interpretation needed, cleaner output
DeepSeek R1 audits the implementation against the original spec — did we actually build what was designed, are there gaps
That last part is huge for business. You end up with a paper trail — here's the design spec, here's the implementation, here's the audit confirming they match. That's basically what enterprise software shops pay consultants a lot of money to produce manually.

It's also great for teams where the designer, developer and QA are different people — each model plays a role, the handoffs are documented automatically in the message pipeline, nothing gets lost in translation between departments.

For your trading bot it's a bit overkill since it's your own project, but if you ever productize any of it — sell the scanner, license the strategy engine — that kind of documented design-build-audit trail becomes a real asset.
